Biography

Welcome to my personal webpage!!!

I am Dr. Vishalkumar Prajapati, currently serving as an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Marwadi University, Rajkot, Gujarat, India. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), Surat, under the supervision of Dr. Ramakanta Meher .

My doctoral research primarily focused on the mathematical modeling and analysis of non-Newtonian hybrid nanofluid flow over stretching surfaces. In addition, I have conducted research in fractional differential equations and mathematical modeling, with applications in fluid dynamics and mathematical biology.

I obtained both my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Mathematics from Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University (HNGU), Patan, Gujarat, India.

I qualified the Joint CSIR-UGC NET examination in Mathematical Sciences with Junior Research Fellowship (AIR 141) and also cleared the Gujarat State Eligibility Test (GSET).

I have published several research articles in international journals indexed in SCIE and Scopus. My research contributions focus on magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) flows, non-Newtonian hybrid nanofluids, and fractional-order mathematical models using advanced semi-analytical and numerical methods.

I have presented my research at several international conferences held in Canada, Poland, and India. I also received International Travel Support from the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, to present my research at an international conference in Poland.

I am a Life Member of the Indian Mathematical Society (IMS) and the Ramanujan Mathematical Society (RMS). I am also a member of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM). My research aims to develop mathematical models and advanced analytical and numerical techniques to better understand complex transport phenomena arising in engineering and applied sciences.

Research Interests

Developing advanced mathematical models, analytical approximations, and high-performance numerical algorithms to study complex transport phenomena, non-Newtonian fluids, and biological systems.

🌊

Fluid Dynamics

Investigating boundary layer flows of nanofluids and non-Newtonian hybrid systems over stretching sheets, incorporating MHD, radiative, and thermophoretic effects.
